1. Why Legacy Models Failed & What Diagnostic Controls Proved

Tracing the transition from coarse-grid discretization artifacts to rigorous single-crystal & disorder bounds

Legacy v1 Model Breakdown
Identified Artifacts
  • Discretization Breakdown: Grid cells $\Delta x = 18\text{--}92\text{ nm}$ ($>15\times \ell_{\mathrm{ex}}$). Severely underestimated exchange; soft cells uncoupled artificially, causing spurious remanence collapse ($M_r/M_s \to 0.40$).
  • Geometric Error: Assumed uniform $t = 60\text{ nm}$ shell with blunt $90^\circ$ rims, assigning massive unphysical magnetic mass to the equator.
  • Bisection Solver Trap: Evaluated negative branch without resaturating, producing an artificial numerical plateau at $\mu_0 H_c = 4.71\text{ T}$ and a $12.2\text{ T}$ ceiling.

  • Rigorous Solver: Monotonic descending sweep eliminating hysteresis trapping.

The Remanence Dilemma
  • Disorder as a Lever: Testing homogeneous $A1$ fraction ($f_{A1} = 0\text{--}66\%$) on sub-exchange mesh ($\Delta x = 1.3\text{ nm}$). Soft grains do lower coercivity ($-7.1\text{ T} / f_{A1}$).
  • The Unbridgeable Trade-off: To drop $H_c \to 1.13\text{ T}$ requires $f_{A1} > 75\%$, which collapses remanence below $M_r/M_s < 0.30$.
  • Reviewer Critique Confirmed: Cannot simultaneously explain $H_c \approx 1.13\text{ T}$ AND high remanence $M_r/M_s \approx 0.64$ with uniform disorder.

Key Scientific Diagnostic Takeaway: Geometry and curvature alone reduce coercivity from the collinear ceiling ($13.2\text{ T}$) to the single-crystal limit ($\sim 6.22\text{ T}$), but cannot reach the experimental $1.13\text{ T}$. Conversely, homogeneous disorder destroys remanence. The solution requires a spatially inhomogeneous order gradient driven by thin-film deposition physics.

The Four Physical Pillars (v2 Architecture)
Multiscale Model (M2/M4)
  • Pillar I (Line-of-Sight Flux): Ballistic cosine thickness profile $t(\theta) = t_0\cos\theta$ ($60\text{ nm}$ at pole $\to 0\text{ nm}$ at equator).
  • Pillar II (Ordering Kinetics Gradient): Thickness-dependent interdiffusion kinetics: $S(\theta) = 0.90\sqrt{\cos\theta}$. In ultra-thin regions ($t < 15\text{ nm}$), surface vacancy sinks and dewetting kinetically suppress $L1_0$ ordering ($S \to 0$).
  • Pillar III (Sub-Exchange Discretization): Mesh $\Delta x = 1.3\text{ nm} < \ell_{\mathrm{ex}} \approx 2.1\text{ nm}$, strictly resolving the domain-wall width ($\delta_w \approx 3.5\text{ nm}$).
  • Pillar IV (300 K Sharrock Thermal Scaling): Thermal activation reduces the coercive barrier by $19.4\%$ for FePt nanograins ($d_g \approx 15\text{ nm}$) over 10 s SQUID sweeps.

• Polar Crown ($\theta \le 60^\circ$): $75\%$ of magnetic mass, $t > 30\text{ nm}$, $S \ge 0.64$ $\implies$ Locks high remanence ($M_r/M_s = 0.637$).
